# Requirements

## Core
- F-01 (must, gate): At-least-once delivery of jobs.
- F-02 (should): Delayed and scheduled jobs.

## Operations
- N-01 (must): Metrics and health endpoints for monitoring.
- N-02 (could): Web dashboard for inspecting jobs.
- N-03 (must, gate): Installs, upgrades and validates its licence with no internet access.

## Cost
- C-01 (must): Predictable pricing at 5 million jobs per month.
- C-02 (should): A usable free tier for development.
